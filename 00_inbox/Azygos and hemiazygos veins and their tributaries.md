### Tributaries of the Azygos System

The system receives blood from posterior region of thorax.

- **Azygos (Right):** Drains into SVC.
- **Hemiazygos (Left Lower):** Drains into Azygos (crosses at T7).
- **Accessory Hemiazygos (Left Upper):** Drains into Azygos.

#### **Parietal Tributaries (Chest Wall & Diaphragm)**

- **Posterior intercostal veins:** These drain the spaces between the ribs into the azygos, hemiazygos, or accessory hemiazygos veins.
- **Right superior intercostal vein:** Drains into the azygos vein.
- **Left superior intercostal vein:** Typically drains into the left brachiocephalic vein.
- **Right and left superior phrenic veins:** Drain the upper surface of the diaphragm.


#### **Visceral Tributaries (Organs)**

These branches drain the internal organs of the mediastinum:
- **Esophageal veins:** Drain the esophagus.
- **Bronchial veins:** Drain the lungs' air passages.
- **Tracheal veins:** Drain the windpipe.
- **Pericardial veins:** Drain the sac surrounding the heart.
