## 3 parts of the oral cavity
- oropharyngeal isthmus
- oral vestibule
- oral cavity proper

#### the roof of the oral cavity(the palate) :
- incisive papilla
- palatine rugae
- palatine raphe
- palatine fevolae
it is divided into two parts *hard and the soft palate*

##### bony base of hard palate
- palatine proc. of maxillae
- horizontal plate of palatine bone
- median and transvers palatine sutures
- incisive canal
- greater and lesser palatine foramina
#### muscles of the soft palate
- levator veli palatini -pharyngeal plexus CN 9to10(11)
- tensor veli palatini - CN5/3
	(these two open the eustachian tube)
- palatoglossus - CN 10
- palatopharyngeus - pharyngeal plexus CN 9to10(11)
- Uvula - CN 10 (CN7)
*all of these fuses to palatine aponeurosis*

#### innervation and blood supply of the palate
- descending palatine a.
- incisive a.
- ascending palatine a.
- greater and lesser palatine n.
- incisive n.
*arteries and nerves of the palate share the same name*

#### the anterolateral wall of the oral cavity
*the lips and cheeks*
1. skin
2. orbicularis oris m. / buccinator m.
3. oral mucosa
