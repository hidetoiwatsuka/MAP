## 3d. Aromatic side chains

|Amino acid|Abbrev.|Ring system|Key feature|
|---|---|---|---|
|Phenylalanine|Phe, F|Benzene|Purely hydrophobic|
|Tyrosine|Tyr, Y|Phenol (−OH on ring)|Phosphorylation site; pKa ~10|
|Tryptophan|Trp, W|Indole (bicyclic)|Largest side chain; UV absorbance|
|Histidine|His, H|Imidazole|pKa ~6; acid-base catalyst|

### Special aromatic interactions

|Interaction|What happens|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**Cation-π**|Cation attracted to π-electron cloud (quadrupole moment of ring)|Acetylcholine (quaternary N⁺) binding to Tyr/Trp in receptors|
|**π-π stacking**|Ring-ring interaction via London dispersion + quadrupole-quadrupole|Tyr or Trp stacking with ATP adenine ring|

**π-π stacking geometries:** Sandwich · T-shaped · Parallel-displaced

## 3e. Acidic side chains & their amides

|Amino acid|Abbrev.|Side chain|pKa-R|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Aspartate|Asp, D|−CH₂COOH|~4|Negative at pH 7.4|
|Glutamate|Glu, E|−CH₂CH₂COOH|~4|Negative at pH 7.4|
|Asparagine|Asn, N|−CH₂CONH₂|—|**Amide** of Asp → neutral|
|Glutamine|Gln, Q|−CH₂CH₂CONH₂|—|**Amide** of Glu → neutral|

> **Asp vs. Asn / Glu vs. Gln:** Same carbon skeleton, but −COOH (ionizable, charged) replaced by −CONH₂ (neutral amide). Common exam trap.
