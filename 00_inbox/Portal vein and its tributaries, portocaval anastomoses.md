It carries blood from your **digestive organs** (stomach, intestines, etc.) directly to your **liver**.

- GI tract absorbs nutrients → nutrient-rich blood drains into the portal vein
- Portal vein enters the liver at the porta hepatis
- Liver processes the blood: metabolizes nutrients, detoxifies substances, stores glycogen, filters pathogens
- Processed blood drains into hepatic veins → IVC → right atrium

### Tributes of the portal vein

- **Superior mesenteric vein**
    - Pancreaticoduodenal veins
    - Pancreatic veins
    - Right gastro-omental vein
    - Jejunal veins
    - Ileal veins
    - Ileocolic vein
    - Right colic vein
    - Middle colic vein
        
- **Inferior mesenteric vein**
    - Left colic vein
    - Sigmoid veins
    - Superior rectal vein
        
- **Splenic vein**
    - Left gastro-omental vein
    - Pancreatic veins
    - Short gastric veins
        
- **Direct tributaries**
    - Cystic vein
    - Left gastric vein with esophageal veins
    - Right gastric vein
    - Posterior superior pancreaticoduodenal vein
    - Prepyloric vein
    - Paraumbilical veins
