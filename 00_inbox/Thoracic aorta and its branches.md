**Thoracic aorta and its branches:**

- Thoracic aorta = the descending aorta from the aortic arch to the aortic hiatus (T11/T12), where it becomes the abdominal aorta

**Visceral branches** (to thoracic organs):

- Bronchial arteries
- Esophageal branches
- Tracheal branches
- Pericardial branches

**Parietal branches** (to chest wall and diaphragm):

- Posterior intercostal arteries
- Right and left superior phrenic arteries

**Key relationships as it descends:**

- Passes initially to the left of the esophagus
- Then moves posterior to the esophagus lower down
- Because of this relationship, an aortic aneurysm can compress the esophagus and cause dysphagia
- Pierces the diaphragm at the aortic hiatus (T11/T12)
