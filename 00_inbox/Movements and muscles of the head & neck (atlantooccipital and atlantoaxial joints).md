
### Craniovertebral Joints

|Joint|Type|Articulation|Movement|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Atlanto-occipital** (paired)|Condylar|Concave superior facets of atlas ↔ convex occipital condyles|Flexion/extension (nodding), slight lateral flexion|
|**Lateral atlantoaxial** (paired)|Planar|Inferior facets of atlas ↔ superior facets of axis|Rotation|
|**Median atlantoaxial** (unpaired)|Pivot|Dens of axis ↔ fovea of atlas + transverse ligament|Rotation (head turning)|
### Craniovertebral Ligaments

|Ligament|Description|
|---|---|
|**Transverse ligament of atlas**|Lateral mass ↔ lateral mass of atlas; holds dens against anterior arch. Most important stabilizer of dens|
|**Cruciate ligament of atlas**|Transverse ligament + superior longitudinal fascicle (→ foramen magnum) + inferior longitudinal fascicle (→ body of C2). Forms a cross shape|
|**Tectorial membrane**|Continuation of posterior longitudinal ligament; body of C2 → internal surface of occipital bone. Covers cruciate ligament posteriorly. Protects spinal cord|
|**Apical ligament of the dens**|Apex of dens → anterior margin of foramen magnum|
|**Alar ligaments**|Sides of dens → medial occipital condyles; limit rotation|
|**Anterior atlanto-occipital membrane**|Anterior arch of atlas → occipital bone|
|**Posterior atlanto-occipital membrane**|Posterior arch of atlas → occipital bone|

**層構造（前→後）:** Apical + alar ligaments → cruciate ligament → tectorial membrane → dura mater / spinal cord

### Suboccipital Muscles

|Muscle|Origin|Insertion|Innervation|Action|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Rectus capitis posterior major**|Spinous process of axis (C2)|Inferior nuchal line (lateral)|Suboccipital nerve (C1)|Extension of head, ipsilateral rotation|
|**Rectus capitis posterior minor**|Posterior tubercle of atlas (C1)|Inferior nuchal line (medial)|Suboccipital nerve (C1)|Extension of head|
|**Obliquus capitis superior**|Transverse process of atlas (C1)|Occipital bone between nuchal lines|Suboccipital nerve (C1)|Extension, lateral flexion of head|
|**Obliquus capitis inferior**|Spinous process of axis (C2)|Transverse process of atlas (C1)|Suboccipital nerve (C1)|Ipsilateral rotation of atlas on axis|

### Prevertebral Neck Muscles

|Muscle|Origin|Insertion|Innervation|Action|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Longus capitis**|Anterior tubercles of transverse processes C3–C6|Basilar part of occipital bone|Anterior rami C1–C3|Flexion of head|
|**Longus colli**|Anterior surfaces C2–T3 bodies and transverse processes|Anterior tubercle of atlas, bodies C2–C4, transverse processes C5–C6|Anterior rami C2–C6|Flexion of cervical spine, slight rotation|
|**Rectus capitis anterior**|Lateral mass of atlas (C1)|Basilar part of occipital bone|Anterior ramus C1|Flexion of head|
|**Rectus capitis lateralis**|Transverse process of atlas (C1)|Jugular process of occipital bone|Anterior ramus C1|Lateral flexion of head|

### Movement summary table

|Movement|Joint|Muscles|
|---|---|---|
|**Flexion**|Atlanto-occipital|Longus capitis, rectus capitis anterior|
|**Extension**|Atlanto-occipital|Rectus capitis posterior major & minor, obliquus capitis superior, semispinalis capitis, splenius capitis, longissimus capitis, spinalis capitis|
|**Lateral flexion**|Atlanto-occipital|Rectus capitis lateralis, obliquus capitis superior, splenius capitis, longissimus capitis|
|**Rotation**|Atlantoaxial|Obliquus capitis inferior (ipsi), rectus capitis posterior major (ipsi), splenius capitis (ipsi), longissimus capitis (ipsi), semispinalis capitis (contra)|
