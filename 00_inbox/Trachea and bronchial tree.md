### Parts of the respiratory system
- Nasal cavity
- Pharynx
- Larynx

- Lower respiratory tract

- Trachea
- Primary bronchi
- Lungs

### Trachea
- starts under cricoid lig.
- made with Anular lig. and Tracheal lig. these make a striped pattern
- it is connected to the primary bronchial tree

### Divisions and parts of the bronchial tree
#### conducting division
- main bronchus
- lobar bronchi
- segmental bronchi
- brionchioles
- terminal bronchioles
#### Respiratory division
- respiratory bronchioles
- alveolar ducts
- pulmonary alveoli
