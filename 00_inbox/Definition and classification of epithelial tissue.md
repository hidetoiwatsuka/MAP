## 2. General Characteristics

### Vascularity

- Epithelia are **avascular** (no blood vessels)
- Nutrients and O₂ are supplied by **diffusion** from capillaries in the underlying connective tissue

### Cell Polarity
![[Pasted image 20260402184238.png]]


|Domain|Location|Features|
|---|---|---|
|**Apical pole**|Faces lumen / external surface|Microvilli, cilia, stereocilia|
|**Lateral domain**|Between neighboring cells|Tight junctions, adherens, desmosomes, gap junctions|
|**Basal pole**|Faces connective tissue|Attached to basement membrane via hemidesmosomes & integrins|

> 💡 「Apical = 管腔側、Basal = 結合組織側」と覚える。基底膜は上皮と結合組織の境界にある薄い構造。

## 3. Basement Membrane

![[Pasted image 20260402184304.png]]


### Definition

- A thin, specialized extracellular structure at the basal surface of all epithelia
- Located at the boundary between epithelium and underlying connective tissue

### Functions

- **Structural support** — anchors epithelium to connective tissue
- **Semipermeable filter** — selective permeability based on size and charge (e.g., renal glomerulus blocks albumin)
- **Regulation of cell behavior** — influences migration, differentiation, proliferation
- **Barrier against invasion** — cancer cells breaking through BM = **invasion** = hallmark of malignancy

> 💡 腎糸球体ではBMがフィルター。がん細胞がBMを突破 = 浸潤 = 悪性の指標。

### Visualization

- **PAS stain** → magenta/pink (most commonly used)
- **Silver stain (reticulin)** → highlights reticular fibers
- Poorly visible on routine H&E

### Structure — Two Layers

```
Basement Membrane
├── Basal lamina ← produced by epithelial cells
│   ├── Lamina lucida (= lamina rara) — pale, adjacent to cell membrane
│   └── Lamina densa — electron-dense core
└── Reticular lamina ← produced by fibroblasts (connective tissue)
```

> 💡 Basal lamina = 上皮細胞が産生、Reticular lamina = 線維芽細胞が産生 → 起源が異なる

### Molecular Components — Basal Lamina

|Molecule|Role|
|---|---|
|**Type IV collagen**|Forms mesh-like network (NOT fibers) — structural scaffold|
|**Laminin**|Cross-shaped glycoprotein; links epithelial cell (via integrins) to basal lamina. First ECM protein in embryonic development|
|**Nidogen (= Entactin)**|Bridging protein; connects laminin network to Type IV collagen network|
|**Perlecan**|Heparan sulfate proteoglycan; provides **negative charge** → repels negatively charged proteins like albumin (filtration)|

> 💡 Type IV collagenは繊維を作らずメッシュ状ネットワーク。Lamininは発生で最初に出現するECMタンパク。

### Other Collagens

|Collagen|Location|Function|
|---|---|---|
|**Type III**|Reticular lamina|Forms reticular fibers — supportive mesh on connective tissue side|
|**Type VII**|Between basal & reticular lamina|Forms **anchoring fibrils** — physically anchors basal lamina to connective tissue. Critical in skin|

> 💡 Type VII欠損 → 皮膚が剥がれる → **Epidermolysis bullosa（表皮水疱症）**

### Related Structures

- **Lamina propria** — loose connective tissue underlying the epithelium of hollow organs (GI, respiratory, urinary)
    - Epithelium + lamina propria (+ muscularis mucosae) = **mucosa**
- **Papillae** — finger-like projections of connective tissue that interdigitate with epithelium → increase contact area
    - Dermal papillae (skin; responsible for fingerprints)
    - Lingual papillae (tongue; some contain taste buds)

## 4. Cell Junctions

### Overview — 5 Types

|Junction|Connects|Function|Transmembrane Protein|Cytoskeleton|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Tight (Zonula occludens)**|Cell ↔ Cell|Barrier / Seal|Occludin, Claudin, ZO|Actin|
|**Adherens (Zonula adherens)**|Cell ↔ Cell|Adhesion belt|E-cadherin + catenins|Actin|
|**Desmosome (Macula adherens)**|Cell ↔ Cell|Strong spot-weld|Desmoglein, Desmocollin|**Intermediate filaments**|
|**Hemidesmosome**|Cell ↔ BM|Anchor to BM|Integrin (α6β4)|**Intermediate filaments**|
|**Gap junction**|Cell ↔ Cell|Communication|Connexin → connexon|None|

> 💡 上4つ = 物理的接続、Gap = 通信チャネル。Hemidesmosomeだけが細胞–基底膜間。

### Detailed Breakdown

#### Tight Junction

- **Claudins** = main family forming the seal; different types determine permeability
- **Occludin** = first tight junction protein discovered; maintains barrier integrity
- **ZO-1/2/3** = intracellular scaffold proteins → link to actin cytoskeleton
- Different claudin expression in kidney segments controls selective ion reabsorption
- "Zonula" = belt-like, encircles the entire cell

> 💡 Claudinの種類によって透過性が変わる。腎臓の各部位で異なるclaudinがイオンの選択的再吸収を制御。

#### Adherens Junction

- **E-cadherin** = Ca²⁺-dependent adhesion molecule (homophilic binding)
- **Catenins (α, β, p120)** → β-catenin binds E-cadherin → α-catenin links to actin
- Loss of E-cadherin → **EMT (epithelial-mesenchymal transition)** → associated with cancer metastasis

> 💡 E-cadherinはCa²⁺依存性。EDTAでCa²⁺除去 → 接着喪失。E-cadherin喪失 = EMT = がん転移に関連。

#### Desmosome

- **Desmoglein + Desmocollin** = cadherin family (Ca²⁺-dependent); interlock between adjacent cells
- **Desmoplakin + Plakoglobin** = adapter proteins linking to **intermediate filaments (keratins)**
- "Macula" = spot-like adhesion (vs belt-like zonula)
- Abundant in tissues under mechanical stress (skin, cardiac muscle)
- 🏥 **Pemphigus vulgaris** — autoantibodies against desmoglein → loss of cell-cell adhesion → blistering

> 💡 Desmosomeはintermediate filamentsに接続 → 引っ張り力に強い。Pemphigus = desmoglein自己抗体 → 水疱。

#### Hemidesmosome

- **Integrin α6β4** → binds to **laminin** in the basal lamina
- Linked to **intermediate filaments (keratins)** inside the cell
- "Hemi-" = half a desmosome; anchors cell to BM (the "floor")
- 🏥 **Bullous pemphigoid** — autoantibodies against hemidesmosome components → epithelium separates from BM → blistering

> 💡 Desmosome = 細胞間のリベット、Hemidesmosome = 細胞と床（BM）のリベット。

#### Gap Junction

- **Connexin** × 6 → **connexon** (hemichannel) → two connexons dock → complete channel
- Allows passage of small molecules (<1.5 kDa), ions, second messengers (Ca²⁺, cAMP, IP₃)
- In cardiac muscle: ions flow through gap junctions → electrical excitation propagates → coordinated contraction

> 💡 Gap junctionは物理的接着ではなく「通信トンネル」。心筋の協調収縮の理由。

### Junctional Complex — Spatial Arrangement (apical → basal)

```
Lumen (apical)
─────────────────
1. Tight junction      ← most apical
2. Adherens junction   ← just below
3. Desmosome           ← spot-welds scattered below
─────────────────
4. Gap junctions       ← various levels along lateral membrane
─────────────────
Basement Membrane
5. Hemidesmosome       ← at the very bottom
```

> 💡 **TAD** = Tight → Adherens → Desmosome の順番で覚える（EM問題頻出）

## 5. Apical Surface Specializations

|Feature|Microvilli|Stereocilia|Cilia|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Core**|**Actin** filaments|**Actin** filaments|**Microtubules (9+2)**|
|**Motile?**|No|No|Yes|
|**Length**|~1–2 μm|Up to 120 μm|~5–10 μm|
|**Motor protein**|None|None|**Dynein**|
|**Cross-linkers**|Fimbrin, **Villin**|Fimbrin, **Espin**|Nexin, Radial spokes|
|**Function**|↑Surface area (absorption)|Absorption / Mechanosensation|Movement of fluid/mucus|
|**Location**|Small intestine, Kidney PCT|Epididymis, Inner ear|Trachea, Uterine tube, Ependyma|

> 💡 最重要: **Actin (microvilli & stereocilia) vs Microtubules (cilia)** — 試験で繰り返し問われる

### Microvilli — Details

![[Pasted image 20260402184352.png]]

- 20–30 parallel actin filaments per microvillus
- Cross-linked by **Fimbrin** (tight bundling) and **Villin** (unique to microvilli; immunohistochemical marker)
- Anchored into the **terminal web** (actin + spectrin meshwork at apical cytoplasm)
- Individual microvilli not visible under light microscopy → collectively appear as **brush border**
- **Villin** is used clinically as a tumor marker for intestinal-origin tumors

> 💡 光顕では個々のmicrovilliは見えない。全体が「brush border」として見える。Villinは小腸腫瘍マーカー。

### Stereocilia — Details

- Very long microvilli — **NOT true cilia** despite the name; do NOT move
- Core = actin filaments (same as microvilli, just much longer)
- Cross-linked by **Fimbrin** and **Espin**
- No 9+2 microtubule arrangement
- Epididymis: fluid absorption to concentrate sperm
- Inner ear hair cells: bending of stereocilia opens ion channels → mechanosensory transduction (sound → electrical signal)

> 💡 名前に"cilia"とあるが中身はactin。「stereociliaの構成は？」→ 答えはactin（microtubuleではない）。

### Cilia — Axoneme (9+2)

![[Pasted image 20260402184407.png]]

|Component|Function|
|---|---|
|**9 outer doublets** (A-tubule complete 13 protofilaments + B-tubule incomplete 10-11)|Structural scaffold|
|**Central pair** (2 singlet microtubules)|Beat pattern regulation|
|**Dynein arms** (outer + inner, on A-tubule)|Motor — uses ATP → generates sliding force → bending|
|**Nexin links** (elastic, between adjacent doublets)|Resist sliding → convert sliding into bending|
|**Radial spokes** (doublets → central pair)|Signal transduction from central pair to dynein|
|**Basal body** (= centriole, 9+0 triplet arrangement)|Anchors and nucleates the cilium at its base|

- **Mucociliary escalator** — coordinated ciliary beating in trachea/bronchi moves mucus + trapped particles upward
- Uterine tube — moves oocyte toward uterus
- Ependymal cells — circulates CSF

> 🏥 **Kartagener syndrome (Primary ciliary dyskinesia)**
> 
> - Genetic defect in **dynein** → immotile cilia
> - Chronic bronchitis / sinusitis (mucus cannot be cleared)
> - Male infertility (sperm tail = 9+2 structure)
> - **Situs inversus** (nodal cilia dysfunction → randomized L-R axis determination)

> 💡 「dynein欠損 → Kartagener → situs inversus + 不妊 + 呼吸器感染」セット暗記。試験超頻出。

## 6. Types of Epithelia

### Classification: Layers × Shape

```
├── Layers: Simple (1 layer) | Stratified (2+ layers) | Pseudostratified
└── Shape:  Squamous | Cuboidal | Columnar | Transitional
```

### Simple Epithelia — all cells rest on BM and reach the apical surface

|Type|Location|Function|
|---|---|---|
|**Simple Squamous**|Endothelium (blood vessel lining), Mesothelium (serous membranes: pericardium, pleura, peritoneum)|Diffusion, pinocytosis, secretion|
|**Simple Cuboidal**|Ovarian surface (germinal epithelium), thyroid follicles|Covering, secretion|
|**Simple Columnar**|Intestine, gallbladder|Protection, absorption, secretion|

> 💡 Endothelium（血管内皮）と Mesothelium（中皮）= どちらもsimple squamousの特別な名前。試験頻出。

### Stratified Epithelia — named by the shape of the **most superficial layer**

|Type|Location|Key Features|
|---|---|---|
|**Stratified Squamous Keratinized**|Epidermis (skin)|Surface cells dead, filled with keratin → tough, waterproof barrier|
|**Stratified Squamous Nonkeratinized**|Mouth, esophagus, larynx, vagina, anal canal|Surface cells nucleated and alive → moist surfaces subject to abrasion|
|**Stratified Cuboidal**|Sweat gland ducts, developing ovarian follicles|Rare; usually only 2 layers|
|**Stratified Columnar**|Conjunctiva of the eye|Rare|

> 💡 Keratinized = 乾燥環境（皮膚）、Nonkeratinized = 湿潤環境（口腔・食道・膣）。

#### Layers of Epidermis (deep → superficial: **B-S-G-L-C**)

![[Pasted image 20260402184442.png]]
![[Pasted image 20260402184452.png]]

|Layer|Features|Mnemonic|
|---|---|---|
|**Stratum basale**|Stem cells, active mitosis, melanocytes|Base = production factory|
|**Stratum spinosum**|Thickest layer, desmosomes, Langerhans cells|Spiny = desmosomes|
|**Stratum granulosum**|Keratohyalin granules, lamellar granules (waterproofing lipid secretion)|Granules = keratinization prep|
|**Stratum lucidum**|Pale, homogeneous, no nuclei, **palms & soles only**|Lucid = special sites only|
|**Stratum corneum**|Fully keratinized, dead cells, outermost layer|Cornified = final barrier|

> 💡 深層→表層: **B**aby **S**kin **G**rows **L**ater **C**ompletely

### Transitional Epithelium (Urothelium)

- **Location:** Bladder, ureters, renal calyces (= urinary tract only)
- **Umbrella cells (dome cells)** — rounded when relaxed, flattened when stretched
![[Pasted image 20260402184546.png]]
- Surface contains **uroplakin** protein → barrier preventing urine penetration

> 💡 Transitionalは泌尿器系専用。膀胱膨張時にumbrella cellsが扁平化。Uroplakinが尿浸透バリア。

### Pseudostratified Epithelium

- **Appears** multilayered, but **all cells touch the BM**
- Not all cells reach the apical surface → nuclei at different levels → illusion of stratification
- **Location:** Trachea, bronchi, nasal cavity
- **Function:** Protection, secretion, mucociliary transport
- **Pseudostratified ciliated columnar epithelium with goblet cells** = longest epithelium name in histology

> 💡 「偽」重層。全細胞がBMに接触。核の高さバラバラ → 多層に見える。

## 7. Glands

### Exocrine vs Endocrine

|a|Exocrine|Endocrine|
|---|---|---|
|**Duct**|Present|Absent (ductless) — lost during development|
|**Product**|Enzymes, mucus, sweat, sebum, etc.|Hormones|
|**Delivery**|Via ducts to body surface or lumen|Into blood / interstitial fluid → systemic|
|**Examples**|Exocrine pancreas, salivary glands, sweat glands|Thyroid, adrenal glands, islets of Langerhans|

- Both originate from epithelial invagination during development
- Exocrine glands maintain duct connection; endocrine glands lose ducts, acquire rich capillary networks

> 💡 膵臓は両方持つ: 外分泌部(acinar→消化酵素→duct→十二指腸) + 内分泌部(islets→insulin/glucagon→血中)。試験頻出。

---

### Axis 1: Structural Classification

**Two parameters:**

- Duct branching: **Simple** (unbranched) vs **Compound** (branched)
- Secretory portion shape: **Tubular** vs **Acinar/Alveolar** vs **Tubuloacinar**

![[Pasted image 20260402184620.png]]

#### Simple Glands

|Type|Shape|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|Simple Tubular|Straight tube|Intestinal crypts (of Lieberkühn)|
|Branched Tubular|Branched tubes → 1 duct|Uterine glands, gastric glands|
|Coiled Tubular|Coiled tube|Eccrine sweat glands|
|Acinar (Alveolar)|Round sac|Small mucous glands of urethra|
|Branched Acinar|Multiple sacs → 1 duct|Sebaceous glands|

#### Compound Glands

|Type|Shape|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|Compound Tubular|Elongated tubes|Brunner's glands (duodenal submucosa)|
|Compound Acinar|Round sacs|Exocrine pancreas|
|Compound Tubuloacinar|Both tubes and sacs|Salivary glands|

#### Quick Reference

|a|Tubular|Acinar|Tubuloacinar|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Simple**|Colon, Stomach, Sweat glands|Urethra, Sebaceous glands|(rare)|
|**Compound**|Brunner's glands|Exocrine pancreas|Salivary glands|

> 💡 Simple = ductが1本道、Compound = 枝分かれする高速道路のインターチェンジ

### Axis 2: Modes of Secretion

![[Pasted image 20260402184648.png]]

| Mode          | Cell Damage         | What Released                           | Key Example                       |                                      |
| ------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Merocrine** | None (exocytosis)   | Vesicle contents only                   | Pancreas, Salivary, Eccrine sweat | ![[Pasted image 20260402184707.png]] |
| **Holocrine** | Entire cell dies    | Whole cell = secretion                  | **Sebaceous glands**              | ![[Pasted image 20260402184718.png]] |

> 💡 **M**erocrine = **M**ild（穏やか）、**H**olocrine = **H**olocaust（全滅）、**A**pocrine = **A**pical pinch-off

### Goblet Cells — Unicellular Glands

![[Pasted image 20260402184843.png]]

- Individual, isolated mucus-secreting cells scattered among other epithelial cells
- **Not** a gland structure — they are **unicellular glands**
- Apical portion: swollen with mucus granules → goblet (wine glass) shape
- Basal portion: narrow, compressed nucleus + RER
- Secretion mechanism: **merocrine (exocytosis)**
- Location: small & large intestine, trachea & bronchi (respiratory + GI tract)
- H&E: pale/empty (mucus washes out) → PAS: **magenta/pink**

> 💡 H&Eで「白く抜けた杯状の空間」。PAS染色でピンクに染まるのがgoblet cellの確認法。

## 8. High-Yield Clinical Correlations

|Condition|Mechanism|
|---|---|
|**Epidermolysis bullosa**|Type VII collagen (anchoring fibrils) deficiency → skin detachment|
|**Pemphigus vulgaris**|Autoantibodies against desmoglein → loss of cell-cell adhesion → blistering|
|**Bullous pemphigoid**|Autoantibodies against hemidesmosome components → epithelium separates from BM → blistering|
|**Kartagener syndrome**|Dynein defect → immotile cilia → respiratory infections + infertility + situs inversus|
|**Cancer invasion**|Tumor cells breaking through BM = invasion = hallmark of malignancy|
|**EMT / Metastasis**|Loss of E-cadherin → epithelial-mesenchymal transition → cancer metastasis|
