## Types of Bone

**Long bones** – tubular bones of the limbs

**Short bones** – e.g., carpal and tarsal bones

**Flat bones** – e.g., scapula, ilium, bones of the calvaria

**Irregular bones** – e.g., vertebrae, anomalous/supernumerary bones not consistently present (skull base)

**Pneumatic bones** – contain air-filled spaces; e.g., bones of the facial skeleton and paranasal sinuses

**Sesamoid bones** – incorporated in tendons; e.g., patella

**Accessory bones** – anomalous/supernumerary; e.g., calvaria and foot (result from failure of fusion of adjacent ossification centers)
