| **Tissue**     | **Cells**                                       | **ECM**             | **Main Function**                                                 |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Epithelial** | Tightly packed **polygonal cells**              | Very little         | Covering and lining surfaces; **secretion and absorption**        |
| **Connective** | Various **fixed and migratory cells**           | Abundant            | **Structural support, protection, transport, and immune defense** |
| **Muscle**     | **Elongated contractile cells (muscle fibers)** | Minimal to moderate | **Contraction and generation of force**                           |
| **Nervous**    | **Neurons and glial cells**                     | Very little         | **Transmission and processing of electrical signals**             |
