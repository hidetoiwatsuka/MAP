
## 5.1 The peptide bond

> **One-liner:** Amino acids link head-to-tail via **peptide (amide) bonds** — condensation between α-amino and α-carboxyl groups.

```
  R₁  O    R₂  O    R₃  O    R₄
  |   ‖    |   ‖    |   ‖    |
⁺H₃N-Cα—C—NH—Cα—C—NH—Cα—C—NH—Cα—COO⁻
         ↑         ↑         ↑
     peptide    peptide   peptide
     bond #1   bond #2   bond #3
```

### Peptide bond properties

|Property|Detail|Why it matters|
|---|---|---|
|**Partial double bond**|C−N has ~40% double bond character (resonance)|Restricts rotation around C−N|
|**Planar**|6 atoms (Cα, C=O, N−H, Cα) lie in a **rigid plane**|Backbone = chain of rigid planes connected by hinges|
|**Trans preferred**|R groups on opposite sides of the bond|Less steric clash than cis|
|**Cis rare**|Only significant before **Proline**|Pro's cyclic structure reduces the cis/trans energy gap|

### The two rotatable bonds (the "hinges")

|Bond|Symbol|Between atoms|Freedom|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Phi (Φ)**|Φ|N — Cα|Free rotation|
|**Psi (Ψ)**|Ψ|Cα — C(=O)|Free rotation|

→ Rotation around Φ and Ψ generates **many possible backbone conformations**, but most are **sterically forbidden**.

## 5.2 The Ramachandran Plot

> **One-liner:** A (Φ, Ψ) scatter plot for every residue — reveals sterically allowed conformations and maps to secondary structures.

### How to read it

- **Each dot** = one amino acid residue, plotted at its (Φ, Ψ) values
- **Allowed regions** = where dots cluster (no atomic clashes)
- **Forbidden regions** = empty areas (steric collisions)

### The two major clusters

|Cluster|Location on plot|Approximate (Φ, Ψ)|Secondary structure|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Lower-left**|Φ ~ −60°, Ψ ~ −45°|(−60°, −45°)|**α-helix**|
|**Upper-left**|Φ ~ −120°, Ψ ~ +135°|(−120°, +135°)|**β-sheet**|

- Different proteins show different α-helix vs. β-sheet ratios → distinct Ramachandran patterns
- Detailed secondary structures → **Lecture 2**

## 5.3 Primary structure

> **One-liner:** Primary structure = the **linear amino acid sequence** of a polypeptide, encoded by DNA.

### Chain length nomenclature

|Term|# of amino acids|
|---|---|
|Dipeptide|2|
|Tripeptide|3|
|Oligopeptide|< ~50|
|Polypeptide|≥ ~50|

### Convention

- Written **N-terminus (left) → C-terminus (right)**
- Numbered starting from the **N-terminus**
- Example: H₃N⁺–Glu₁–Ala₂–Lys₃–Gly₄–COO⁻ = **EAKG**

|Terminus|Group|Charge at pH 7.4|
|---|---|---|
|**N-terminus**|Free α-amino (−NH₃⁺)|Positive|
|**C-terminus**|Free α-carboxyl (−COO⁻)|Negative|

> **Primary structure is the foundation:** It determines all higher-order structure (secondary → tertiary → quaternary) through the physicochemical properties of its amino acid sequence.
