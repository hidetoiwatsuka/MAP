### **Prenatal Circulation**

- **Gas exchange occurs in the placenta**, not the lungs (fetal lungs are unexpanded and minimally perfused)
- **Umbilical vein** (unpaired) delivers O₂/nutrient-rich blood from placenta to fetus
- **Ductus venosus** — venovenous shunt near the liver; umbilical vein → IVC (bypasses hepatic sinusoids)
- Umbilical vein also anastomoses with **portal vein** → liver for metabolic processing
- **Heart has a right-to-left shunt**:
    - IVC blood → right atrium → passes through **foramen ovale** (guided by valve of IVC) → left atrium
    - SVC blood → right ventricle → pulmonary trunk → **ductus arteriosus** → aorta (bypasses lungs)
- **Umbilical arteries** (paired, from internal iliac aa.) return deoxygenated blood to placenta
- Pulmonary veins carry very little blood to the left atrium (because lungs are barely perfused)
