> Remember **Vitamin C and D**
> 
> **Mnemonic:** VIT(sit) C(sid) and D
> 
> - VIT – from 1st part
> - C – from 2nd part
> - D – from 3rd part

1. **V**ertebral artery
2. **I**nternal thoracic artery
3. **T**hyrocervical trunk
    - **S**uprascapular artery
    - **I**nferior **thyroid** artery (Thyro-)
    - **T**ransverse **cervical** artery (Cervical)
4. **C**ostocervical trunk
    - **S**uperior **i**ntercostal (Costo-)
    - **D**eep cervical (Cervical)
5. **D**orsal scapular artery

for the right subclavian the origin is from the brachiocephalic trunk. left subclavian origin directly from the aortic arch.
